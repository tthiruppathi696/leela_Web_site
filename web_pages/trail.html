THE LEELA PALACE TRAIL

There is no land quite as soul-stirring as India and no finer way to discover its rich mosaic of culture, heritage, and beauty than through the lens of The Leela. From the shimmering lakes of Udaipur to the tranquil backwaters of Kerala and the vibrant rhythms of New Delhi, each destination reveals a different facet of India's essence.

Welcome to The Leela Palace Trail — an extraordinary journey across eight award-winning palaces, hotels and resorts in India's most captivating locales: New Delhi, Jaipur, Udaipur, Bengaluru, Chennai, Ashtamudi, Kovalam and Hyderabad. Curate your own multi-day itinerary as you traverse this trail of timeless elegance.

Every palace along the trail offers a distinct narrative from architectural marvels and breathtaking surroundings to curated cultural encounters and unparalleled service. At each stop, discover India at its most luxurious, most authentic, and most unforgettable.

This is more than a journey. It is an invitation to experience India through a royal lens only at The Leela.
