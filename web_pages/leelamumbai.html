The Leela Mumbai

∞

The inaugural jewel of The Leela, established in 1986, offers discerning travellers a blend of India’s rich heritage and refined modernity. Just five minutes from the airport, discover an opulent sanctuary nestled within 11 acres of exquisitely landscaped gardens, a haven of tranquillity and elegance.

This luxury hotel in Mumbai lets you savour delicacies recreated from royal kitchens, local specialties, and global cuisines.

On guided outings, discover the diverse tapestry of Mumbai, from ancient temples to high-end shopping malls, hidden nature trails, or a harbour cruise. At sunset, join us for the ceremonial lighting of the Nilavilakku lamp, an act believed to dispel darkness and welcome prosperity.

As the city winds down, unwind with a glass of fine wine and let the gentle breeze carry you into the night. The Leela Mumbai, a renowned 5-Star hotel in Mumbai, is more than a stay — it’s an embodiment of the city’s rich culture, energy, and hospitality.

Celebrated as one of the finest 5-Star hotels in Mumbai, it continues to set the standard for luxury and gracious service.
